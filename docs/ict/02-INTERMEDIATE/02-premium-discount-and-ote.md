## Module 02: Premium vs. Discount & Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

---

### 1. The Dealing Range Framework

Institutions trade according to commercial wholesale principles:
> **Buy at Wholesale (Discount), Sell at Retail (Premium).**

You should never look to buy an asset simply because it is going up, nor short an asset simply because it is falling. First, you must determine what **Dealing Range** the market is currently operating within.

```
 SWING HIGH (Top of Dealing Range)
 ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════  100%
  ▲
  │   PREMIUM ZONE (Expensive)
  │   Institutions look to SHORT / SELL.
  │   Never initiate new longs here.
  │
 ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  50% EQUILIBRIUM
  │
  │   DISCOUNT ZONE (Cheap / Wholesale)
  │   Institutions look to BUY / ACCUMULATE.
  │   Never initiate new shorts here.
  ▼
 ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════  0%
 SWING LOW (Bottom of Dealing Range)
```

#### How to Define a Dealing Range:
1. Identify the most recent significant fractal Swing Low and fractal Swing High that initiated the current impulse leg.
2. Measure the exact price distance from low to high (or high to low).
3. The exact 50% midpoint is **Equilibrium**.

---

### 2. Premium vs. Discount Rules of Engagement

1. **Bullish Bias:** If your higher-timeframe bias is bullish, you **must only look for long entries inside the Discount Zone (< 50% Equilibrium)**. Buying in Premium exposes your stop loss to deep retracements.
2. **Bearish Bias:** If your higher-timeframe bias is bearish, you **must only look for short entries inside the Premium Zone (> 50% Equilibrium)**.
3. **Internal PD Arrays:** An Order Block or Fair Value Gap located in the Discount zone is dramatically more reliable for a long position than one located above 50% Equilibrium.

### 3. The ICT Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

The **Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)** is a calibrated Fibonacci retracement model developed by Michael Huddleston to pinpoint institutional re-accumulation and re-distribution zones.

```
 SWING HIGH ════════════════════════════════════════════════════  1.000 (100%)
                                                                 0.790 (Deep OTE boundary)
       ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             OPTIMAL TRADE ENTRY (OTE)             │
       │   0.705 ── "THE SWEET SPOT"                       │     0.705 (Institutional Sweet Spot)
       └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                                                 0.618 (Standard Fib limit)
 EQUILIBRIUM ───────────────────────────────────────────────────  0.500 (50% Fair Value)
                                                                 0.000 (0% Origin Swing Low)
 SWING LOW  ════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 TARGET 1   --------------------------------------------------- -0.270 (First Expansion Target)
 TARGET 2   =================================================== -0.620 (Full Expansion Target)
```

#### The ICT Fibonacci Settings:
- **0.000:** Anchor / Swing Origin
- **0.500:** Equilibrium
- **0.618:** 62% Retracement (OTE Entry Boundary)
- **0.705:** 70.5% Retracement (**The Institutional Sweet Spot**)
- **0.790:** 79% Retracement (Deep Discount / Stop-hunt threshold)
- **1.000:** Swing Extreme / Invalidation
- **-0.270:** Profit Target 1 (Partial TP)
- **-0.620:** Profit Target 2 (Full Expansion Run)

### 4. Step-by-Step OTE Execution Protocol

#### Bullish OTE Setup:
1. **Identify the Impulse Leg:** Look for a clear fractal Swing Low that pushed aggressively upward with displacement, taking out a previous high (BOS/MSS).
2. **Anchor the Fib Tool:** Draw from the **Swing Low (0.0)** up to the **Swing High (1.0)**.
3. **Wait for Retracement:** Do not chase the green candles. Allow price to pull back below 50% Equilibrium into the **62% – 79% zone**.
4. **Identify Confluence:** Check if there is a **Fair Value Gap (FVG)** or **Order Block (OB)** sitting right inside the **0.705 sweet spot**.
5. **Entry:** Place limit order at 0.705 (or at the top of the nested FVG within OTE).
6. **Stop Loss:** Set safely below the Swing Low origin (1.0 or just past the 0.79 level).
7. **Take Profit Targets:**
   - TP1: The Swing High (100% liquidity sweep).
   - TP2: -0.270 Fibonacci extension.
   - TP3: -0.620 Fibonacci extension.
