### 1. The Anatomy of Market Delivery: Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution

Every financial instrument delivers price across three consecutive algorithmic phases, known as **Power of 3 (PO3)** or **AMD**:

1. **Accumulation (A):** The market consolidates in a defined range near the opening price. Smart money discreetly loads orders while retail traders attempt breakout strategies.
2. **Manipulation (M):** The algorithm creates a violent, deceptive move in the **opposite direction** of the true trend. This move raids stops and traps breakout traders offside. This is the **Judas Swing**.
3. **Distribution (D):** The genuine institutional expansion begins, driving price with conviction toward higher-timeframe liquidity pools or inefficiencies.

### 2. Deconstructing the Daily Candle (OHLC / OLHC)

Traditional technical analysis looks at a daily candle in hindsight. ICT allows you to forecast the candle **as it is forming**:

#### A. Bullish Daily Candle (Open - Low - High - Close: OLHC)
- **Open:** The day opens at 00:00 EST.
- **Low (Manipulation):** During early London (02:00 – 04:00 EST), price dips **below the 00:00 Open**. This forms the lower wick (Low of the Day).
- **High (Distribution):** Price rallies strongly through New York, reaching toward premium targets.
- **Close:** Price pulls back slightly before 16:00 EST, closing near the daily high.

#### B. Bearish Daily Candle (Open - High - Low - Close: OHLC)
- **Open:** The day opens at 00:00 EST.
- **High (Manipulation):** Price rallies **above the 00:00 Open** during London or NY Open. This creates the upper wick (High of the Day).
- **Low (Distribution):** Price plunges throughout the session, targeting discount SSL pools.
- **Close:** Price consolidates into the settlement, closing near the daily low.

> **Key Rule of Engagement:**
> - If you are **bullish**, you want to enter when price is **BELOW the 00:00 Open**.
> - If you are **bearish**, you want to enter when price is **ABOVE the 00:00 Open**.

### 3. The Judas Swing: Detecting Institutional Deception

The **Judas Swing** is named after the biblical betrayal—it appears to offer a clean breakout in one direction, only to violently betray the participants who chased it.

```
                        THE JUDAS SWING TRAP
                        
                ===================================  Asian Session High
                               ▲
                              / \  <-- 02:30 AM EST: Aggressive rally!
                             /   \     Retail screams "Bullish Breakout!"
                            /     \    Orders triggered: Buy Stops.
                           /       \
     Asian Consolidation  /         ▼
     ────────────────────           [DISPLACEMENT COLLAPSE]
     ────────────────────           Price slams back into the range,
                                    breaks structure (MSS),
                                    and initiates a 150-pip downtrend.
```

#### How to Identify a Judas Swing in Real Time:
1. Note the Asian Session High and Low prior to 02:00 AM EST.
2. At the London Open, watch for a swift spike that punches through the Asian high/low.
3. Observe the speed and price action. If the sweep reaches an HTF PD Array (like a 4H Bearish FVG) and fails to hold with candle bodies, watch for the immediate lower-timeframe Market Structure Shift.
4. Enter the reversal as soon as the displacement candle confirms the trap.

### 4. Fractal Nature of PO3

Power of 3 is not restricted to daily candles; it occurs fractally across all time horizons:

| Time Horizon | Accumulation Phase | Manipulation Phase | Distribution Phase |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Candle** | Monday Asian / London | Tuesday / Wednesday Judas | Thursday / Friday expansion |
| **Daily Candle** | Asian session (19:00–00:00) | London Open (02:00–04:00) | New York session (08:30–15:00) |
| **Intraday 15m** | Pre-market range | 09:30 cash open sweep | 09:50–11:00 AM trend run |
