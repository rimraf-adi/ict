# Level 04: Execution Models & Strategies
## Module 02: The ICT Silver Bullet Strategy

---

### 1. Overview: The 60-Minute Precision Weapon

The **ICT Silver Bullet** is a hyper-focused, time-restricted intraday execution model introduced by Michael Huddleston in early 2023. 

It is designed for traders who work day jobs or prefer not to sit in front of charts all day. It restricts all analysis and trade execution to **three strictly defined 60-minute windows** per day, targeting a fixed, reliable payout (10 to 15 handles on ES, 20 to 40 handles on NQ, or 15 to 25 pips on Forex).

```
          THE 3 DAILY SILVER BULLET 60-MINUTE WINDOWS (EST)
          
  [ 03:00 AM – 04:00 AM ]       [ 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM ]       [ 02:00 PM – 03:00 PM ]
      London Open                     New York AM                   New York PM
  European liquidity run        Post-open morning trend       Afternoon settlement
```

---

### 2. The 3 Silver Bullet Trading Windows

#### 1. London Session Silver Bullet: **03:00 AM – 04:00 AM EST**
- Occurs right after the initial London open volatility settles.
- Capitalizes on the Judas Swing reversal or the continuation leg toward the London session high/low.

#### 2. New York AM Silver Bullet: **10:00 AM – 11:00 AM EST** *(Most Popular & Reliable)*
- Occurs immediately after the 09:30 AM equities open shakeout and the 10:00 AM macroeconomic data releases.
- Offers the cleanest, most consistent institutional trends of the trading day.

#### 3. New York PM Silver Bullet: **02:00 PM – 03:00 PM EST**
- Occurs during the afternoon session, often following the midday lunch consolidation.
- Targets daily range completion into the market close.

### 3. Complete Step-by-Step Execution Rules

#### Pre-requisite:
Open your **1-minute or 5-minute chart** exactly as the clock strikes the start of the Silver Bullet hour.

#### Execution Sequence:
1. **Identify the Nearest Liquidity Pool:** At the start of the hour, mark the nearest un-swept session high/low, previous hour high/low, or relative equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL). This is your target objective.
2. **Wait for the Liquidity Run:** Inside the 60-minute window, price will either sweep a structural level or trade directly into a higher-timeframe PD Array.
3. **Spot the Displacement & FVG:** Price must generate a clear displacement move that breaks local market structure and prints a fresh **Fair Value Gap (FVG)**.
4. **Enter on Retracement:** Place a limit order inside the FVG as soon as price retraces.
5. **Target the Objective:**
   - On **ES (S&P 500 Futures):** Target a fixed **10 to 15 handles**.
   - On **NQ (Nasdaq Futures):** Target a fixed **25 to 40 points**.
   - On **EUR/USD or GBP/USD:** Target **15 to 20 pips**.
6. **Hard Cutoff Rule:** If the trade setup does not form within the 60-minute window, **CLOSE YOUR LAPTOP**. Do not force a trade. If you are entered in a trade and it has not hit target by the close of the hour, evaluate managing to breakeven or trailing stops.

### 4. Silver Bullet Setup Checklist

| Checkpoint | Requirement | Verified? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Clock Check** | Is the current time inside 03:00–04:00, 10:00–11:00, or 14:00–15:00 EST? | [ ] |
| **Clear Draw on Liquidity** | Is there an obvious un-swept high/low or FVG within 15–30 points of current price? | [ ] |
| **Displacement** | Did a 1-minute or 5-minute candle displace with conviction and leave an FVG? | [ ] |
| **FVG Retest** | Is price retracing back into the body of the FVG before hitting target? | [ ] |
| **Risk / Reward** | Is your stop-loss placed cleanly behind the swing invalidation point? | [ ] |

### 5. Why the Silver Bullet is Ideal for Funded Account Challenges

Proprietary trading firms (Topstep, Apex, FTMO, FundedNext) enforce strict daily drawdown limits and consistency rules. 

Because the Silver Bullet:
- Avoids choppy midday sessions,
- Requires only 1 trade per day during peak algorithmic volume,
- Features a mathematically asymmetric stop-loss to target ratio ($1:2$ or better),
it has become the #1 chosen system for traders passing institutional evaluation challenges.
