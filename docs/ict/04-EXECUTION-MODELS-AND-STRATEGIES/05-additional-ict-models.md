### 1. The London Day Open Strategy (The Classic Judas Reversal)

#### Target Asset: `EUR/USD`, `GBP/USD`, `DXY`
#### Primary Window: 02:00 AM – 04:30 AM EST

```
                   THE LONDON DAY OPEN PROTOCOL
                   
 1. Mark Asian High & Asian Low (19:00 – 00:00 EST).
 2. Observe 00:00 NY Midnight Open price.
 3. Between 02:00 and 03:00 EST, watch for the Judas Swing:
    - If daily bias is BULLISH: Price plunges BELOW Midnight Open & sweeps Asian Low.
    - If daily bias is BEARISH: Price rallies ABOVE Midnight Open & sweeps Asian High.
 4. Sweep must tap into a Higher Timeframe (H1/H4) PD Array.
 5. Drop to 5-minute / 15-minute chart for MSS with displacement.
 6. Enter at the FVG.
 7. Target: The opposing Asian Range boundary (and beyond into daily targets).
```

### 2. The Asian Range Scalp (Range-Bound Mean Reversion)

#### Target Asset: `EUR/USD`, `USD/JPY`, `AUD/USD`
#### Primary Window: 20:00 PM – 23:30 PM EST

While the Asian session is typically avoided by trend traders, it offers precision mean-reversion setups when the daytime sessions were high-volatility expansion days:

- **Condition:** New York session experienced an enormous directional trend (exhausting daily ATR).
- **Setup:** During early Asian hours, price sweeps the high or low of the early consolidation.
- **Entry:** Enter counter-trend when a 5-minute Market Structure Shift prints, targeting the **50% Equilibrium of the Asian Range**.
- **Objective:** Quick 10–15 pips. Close all positions prior to 00:00 Midnight EST.

### 3. The New York PM Session Expansion Model

#### Target Asset: `ES`, `NQ`, `YM`, `US 10Y Yields`
#### Primary Window: 13:30 PM – 15:45 PM EST

Traders often turn off their screens after the morning session, missing the powerful institutional settlement run:

1. **The Lunch Doldrums (12:00 – 13:30 EST):** Price enters a tight consolidation, often building relative equal highs or lows.
2. **The 13:30 Ignition Macro:** At 13:30, smart money desk traders return from lunch. Price sweeps the lunch consolidation high or low.
3. **The 14:00 PM Silver Bullet:** An FVG prints on the 1-minute or 5-minute chart between 14:00 and 15:00 EST.
4. **Execution:** Ride the late-day expansion into the **15:15 – 15:45 Closing Macro**, targeting the daily high or low of the day.

### 4. The "Bread & Butter" 20-Pip Daily Scalp

Michael Huddleston famously demonstrated that a trader does not need to capture 100-pip moves to generate professional wealth:
> **One 20-pip scalp per day, with proper position sizing (1-2% risk on funded or institutional accounts), provides astronomical compounding over a multi-year horizon.**

#### The Rules of the 20-Pip Setup:
- **Asset:** EUR/USD or GBP/USD.
- **Timeframe:** 15-minute chart for context, 5-minute chart for trigger.
- **Trigger:** Reversal from a 15-minute FVG inside London or NY AM Killzones.
- **Stop Loss:** 10–12 pips.
- **Take Profit:** Fixed 20 pips (or the nearest session liquidity pool).
- **Execution Mandate:** Once 15 pips are gained, move stop to breakeven. Take full exit at 20 pips. Close terminal for the day.
