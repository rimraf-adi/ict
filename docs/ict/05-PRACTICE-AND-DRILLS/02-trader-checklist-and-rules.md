### 1. The Pre-Session Routine (30 Minutes Before Open)

Every institutional and successful retail trader follows a strict pre-flight routine. Never sit down and immediately execute market orders.

```
 [ PRE-SESSION CHECKLIST ]
 
 [ ] 1. Check Economic Calendar (Forex Factory / Bloomberg):
        - Identify Red Folder / High-Impact News (08:30, 10:00, or 14:00 EST).
        - Rule: NEVER hold open positions into a Red Folder release unless in heavy profit.
 
 [ ] 2. Establish Higher Timeframe Daily / 4-Hour Bias:
        - Where is IPDA drawing price? (Is the magnet an Old High, Old Low, or FVG?)
        - Are we currently trading in Premium (>50%) or Discount (<50%)?
 
 [ ] 3. Mark Key Institutional Anchor Levels on Your Chart:
        - [ ] Previous Day High (PDH) & Previous Day Low (PDL)
        - [ ] Previous Week High (PWH) & Previous Week Low (PWL)
        - [ ] New York Midnight Open (00:00 EST)
        - [ ] Asian Session High (ASH) & Asian Session Low (ASL)
        - [ ] New Day / New Week Opening Gaps (NDOG / NWOG)
```

### 2. Live Session Execution Protocol (The 6-Gate System)

Before clicking "Buy" or "Sell", your trade must pass all 6 consecutive gates:

#### Gate 1: Time of Day
- [ ] Is the clock inside an official ICT Killzone (London: 02:00–05:00, NY AM: 08:30–11:00, NY PM: 13:30–15:30 EST)?
- *If NO: Stop. Do not trade.*

#### Gate 2: Liquidity Engagement
- [ ] Has price swept a clear structural liquidity pool (PDH, PDL, Asian high/low, or intraday EQH/EQL)?
- *If NO: You are trading in the middle of a range. Wait for the sweep.*

#### Gate 3: Structural Shift (MSS)
- [ ] Did price aggressively shift market structure with **candle bodies closing across the swing point**?
- *If NO (only wicks pierced the level): It is a raid/continuation, not an MSS. Do not enter.*

#### Gate 4: Displacement Quality
- [ ] Did the shift leave a crisp, energetic Fair Value Gap with large candle bodies and minimal wicks?
- *If NO: Lack of displacement means no institutional sponsorship.*

#### Gate 5: Value Zone
- [ ] If Buying: Is the entry price in the Discount zone (<50% of the impulse leg)?
- [ ] If Selling: Is the entry price in the Premium zone (>50% of the impulse leg)?
- *If NO: Do not buy in premium or short in discount.*

#### Gate 6: Risk-to-Reward Ratio
- [ ] Is the protective stop loss placed strictly behind the invalidation extreme?
- [ ] Does the trade provide a minimum mathematical Risk-to-Reward of **1:2 or better** to the objective target?
- *If all 6 Gates are YES: Execute the order without hesitation.*

### 3. Risk Management Non-Negotiables

No trading strategy will survive without ironclad capital preservation:

1. **Max Risk Per Trade (0.5% to 1.0% Rule):** Never risk more than 1% of your total account balance on a single trade. If your account is \$50,000, your maximum loss on a trade must not exceed \$500.
2. **The "Two Strikes and You're Out" Rule:** If you take two consecutive losses in a single day, shut down your charting platform immediately. Market conditions are likely in a consolidation/whipsaw profile.
3. **Daily Loss Circuit Breaker (-2% Max):** If your daily drawdown reaches 2%, you are banned from trading until the next calendar day.
4. **Moving to Breakeven:** Once price reaches **$1:1$ or takes out the first intermediate liquidity pool**, move your stop-loss to Breakeven (entry price) and take off a partial position (e.g. 50%). Let the remainder run risk-free to the final draw on liquidity.

### 4. Daily Post-Market Journaling Rubric

At the end of every trading session, log the following in your trade journal:
- **Screenshot 1:** Higher-timeframe context (Daily / 4-Hour).
- **Screenshot 2:** Lower-timeframe execution (1-minute / 5-minute) with entry, stop loss, and exit marked.
- **Answer the 4 Post-Trade Questions:**
  1. *Did I follow my plan and wait for the Killzone/Macro?*
  2. *Was my Draw on Liquidity accurate?*
  3. *Did I experience FOMO or emotional impulse?*
  4. *What did IPDA teach me today?*
